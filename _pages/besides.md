<div class="prose" markdown="1">
I never identify myself as a "pure CS student." I'm a bit "artistic" - reflected in my pursuit of aesthetics (including this personal website, where I did some custom design rather than using the original template :), as well as in my hobbies. I firmly believe in all-round development.

I'm an amateur pianist who started my journey at the age of 7. I once dreamed of becoming a professional pianist, but after failing to get into the Music Middle School Affiliated to the Shanghai Conservatory of Music, I let go of that dream and ended up in STEM. Despite the frustration, my love for piano has never faded. I have performed at many concerts both on and off campus. For me, piano is not merely a hobby - it's a way of life. My ultimate dream is to perform Rachmaninoff's 3rd Piano Concerto - one of the most demanding masterpieces in the repertoire - with a professional orchestra!

#### Prizes:

- **First Prize**, 2026 The Ninth Kawai Asia Piano Competetion (Grand Final, Chinese Works – Adult Group)
- **4th Place**, 2026 The Ninth Kawai Asia Piano Competetion (Preliminary in Shenzhen, Chinese Works – Adult Group, 1st to 3rd places were vacant)
- **Honorable Mention in Grand Final**, 2026 Shenzhen Piano Open Competition (Ranked 7th in Free Choice Group A)
- **Honorable Mention in Grand Final**, 2026 Shenzhen Piano Open Competition (Ranked 7th in Amateur Concerto Group)
- **5th Place**, 2025 Shenzhen Piano Open Competition (Amateur Group I, 1st and 2nd places were vacant)
- **First Prize**, Art Festival of Wenzhou, 2021

#### Representative Concerts:

- Midsummer Concert of SUSTech, 2025 - Performed Beethoven's Third Movement of the Triple Concerto with a string orchestra!
- Shenzhen Concert Hall "Music Afternoon Tea" Concert, 2024
- Shenzhen Concert Hall Steinway Garden Concert, 2024
- 11th Shenzhen Piano Festival, Concert of Social Art Education Achievements, 2023

I started going to gym in August 2026, I'm still a novice in strength training : )

In addition, I enjoy talking with different people from all backgrounds. Feel free to reach out for an online chat for anything (Academics, personal career, or just casual chat about daily life!)
